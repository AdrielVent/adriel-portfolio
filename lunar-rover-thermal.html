Requirements I wrote

I wrote 10 thermal control requirements (THM IDs) for the System Requirements Review, including a 480-hour lunar-night survival case, battery temperature limits of 0 to +15 °C operating and −10 to +25 °C survival, and avionics limits of −20 to +60 °C.

Following System Requirements Review feedback (RFA-04), I revised the 10 requirements to be solution-neutral and verifiable.

Risk I owned (R7)

Heater power was still TBR, so I bounded it and logged the worst case as a risk (one of 4 mission risks I owned out of 16 total) and set mitigation plans with the power team.

Procurement & budget

I planned thermal procurement and lead times from public NASA contract data (multilayer insulation ~15 weeks, heaters ~9 weeks) and co-built the $97K thermal budget.

Mission statement & ConOps

I wrote the Mission Concept Review mission statement, which NASA L'SPACE reviewers scored 24/25 with no RFAs. I also wrote the Mission Operations concept of operations (ConOps): landing checkout, sunlit-to-PSR radiation-mapping loop, and 480-hour lunar-night hibernation on battery-powered heaters.
