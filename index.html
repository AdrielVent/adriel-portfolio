  2. Multi-diameter O-ring cutting fixture

    Stellant Systems co-op · 2026 · text only

    Cutting O-rings to several diameters had a blade-deflection problem that made cuts hard to repeat. I designed the fixture and printed four FDM iterations, changing each one based on feedback from the technicians who use it, and applied GD&T and a tolerance stack-up to deal with blade deflection. I don't have a before/after measurement of cut quality, so I don't claim one.

    Company drawings and photos aren't public. Described here with my manager's OK.
