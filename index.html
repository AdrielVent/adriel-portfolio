  • Thermal model troubleshooting

    Stellant Systems co-op · 2026

    Problem & Resolution. Troubleshot a non-physical 600 °C hot spot in an ANSYS thermal model: traced it to a missing thermal interface material (TIM) layer, revised contact conductance from vendor data, and re-meshed; the prediction landed within 2–3 °C of test thermocouple readings. Cut predicted junction temperature of a power-supply module 57.6 °C (122.5 to 64.9 °C) with a thermal gap pad; evaluated aluminum vs. copper heat spreaders and recommended aluminum for mass and cost.

    Described in words only, with my manager's OK. Company drawings and photos aren't public.

Heat sink for a 100 W CPU

ENGG 114 Heat Transfer, Hofstra · individual project · Spring 2026

Objective
Keep a 50.8 mm CPU at or below 85 °C while it dissipates 100 W in 25 °C air, on the existing mounts.
My contribution
All of it: concept screening, a MATLAB resistance model, CAD, Fusion 360 thermal study, drawings and report.
Result
A 15-fin Al-6061 design predicted at 69.85 °C (MATLAB). Not built or bench tested; the case study shows where the prediction could be wrong.

  • Lunar rover thermal control

    NASA L'SPACE Mission Concept Academy · Thermal Subsystem, Team 6 · Sep 2026 – Dec 2026

    A student academy, not NASA employment. My contribution. Wrote the Mission Concept Review mission statement (reviewers scored it 24/25, no RFAs), the Mission Operations ConOps, and 10 thermal requirements including a 480-hour lunar-night survival case, revised to be solution-neutral after SRR review. Owned 4 of 16 mission risks and flagged a heater energy shortfall (8,064 Wh needed across worst-case survival vs. 7,124 Wh battery capacity; bounded across 4,987 / 6,072 / 8,064 / 7,147 Wh cases). Planned MLI and heater lead times from public NASA contract data, co-built the $97K thermal budget, and drew the SRR thermal zoning schematic.

  • Multi-diameter O-ring cutting fixture & silicone seal tooling

    Stellant Systems co-op · 2026 · Autodesk Inventor · GD&T · FDM

    Problem. Cutting O-rings to several diameters had blade-deflection issues causing inconsistent sealing cuts. My contribution. Designed the fixture in Autodesk Inventor across four FDM iterations with technician ergonomic feedback, applying ASME Y14.5 GD&T and RSS tolerance stack-up analysis; documented the design in a first Engineering Change Notice (ECN) now in shop-floor use. Additionally redesigned a silicone gasket for altitude-chamber vacuum leak tests (groove-matched profile, 3D-printed mold, degassed liquid silicone) and iterated a 3D-printed syringe-dispensing tool.
