Strongest documentation first

Heat sink for a 100 W CPU

ENGG 114 Heat Transfer, Hofstra · individual project · Spring 2026

Objective
Keep a 50.8 mm CPU at or below 85 °C while it dissipates 100 W in 25 °C air, on the existing mounts.
My contribution
All of it: concept screening, a MATLAB resistance model, CAD, Fusion 360 thermal study, drawings and report.
Result
A 15-fin Al-6061 design predicted at 69.85 °C (MATLAB). Not built or bench tested; the case study shows where the prediction could be wrong.

  • Multi-diameter O-ring cutting fixture

    Stellant Systems co-op · 2026

    Problem. Cutting O-rings to several diameters had a blade-deflection problem that made cuts hard to repeat. My contribution. I designed the fixture and printed four FDM iterations, changing each one based on feedback from the technicians who use it, and applied GD&T and a tolerance stack-up to deal with blade deflection. Status. Built and iterated with the floor. I have no before/after measurement of cut quality, so I don't claim one.

    Described in words only, with my manager's OK. Company drawings and photos aren't public.

  • Structural FEA: C-frame and three parts

    Coursework · SOLIDWORKS static FEA

    Problem. Remove material from a cast-steel C-frame under a 45,000 N load without losing its safety margin. Result. After iterating the geometry, a 1.41 factor of safety with 15% less volume. I also ran static studies on a table, an L-bracket and a shaft collar. Mesh and convergence details are not in these images.

Reviewers scored it 24/25 with no requests for action. The team's descope, replacing the radioisotope heater with electric heaters, was accepted. I earned the academy's Requirements skill badge (Sep 2026).
